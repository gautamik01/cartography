I trained as an architect in Mumbai, then studied urbanism and landscape planning at KU Leuven. I spent a few years designing parks, campuses, townships and even an airport landscape in India.

These days I'm at Penn, in the Master of Urban Spatial Analytics. Most of my week goes into maps and interactive prototypes, built in Python and GIS, in 2D and 3D. Some grow into small web apps like Raasta, which finds you the nicer walk instead of the quickest one. Others become interactive maps, like my ski guide for first-timers leaving New York or Philly.

What I enjoy most is playing with urban data and generative AI. I like vibe-coding prototypes with clean interfaces, grounded in real data, that make getting around a city feel easier and more human.

Away from the screen, you'll find me hiking, gardening, taking photos or brewing something at home. I also run and swim. And I'm always on the lookout for a good beer.
